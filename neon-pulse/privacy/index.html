Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Pulse — Cyber Survivor (“Neon Pulse”, “the game”, “we”, “us”), a mobile game for iOS and Android developed and published by independent developer Talha Coşkun, collects, uses, shares and protects information when you play the game. If you have any questions, contact us at devsupport.tc@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You can play without registering — the game creates an anonymous account automatically.
  • Linking a Google account or Apple Game Center is optional.
  • Ads are rewarded ads only and are shown only when you choose to watch one.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we never receive your payment card details.
  • Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device — no server-side tracking.
  • You can reset your progress in the game and request full account and data deletion by email at any time.

1.Information we collect

1.1 Information created when you play

  • Anonymous account ID — a random user identifier created by Firebase Authentication the first time you open the game. It is not linked to your real name.
  • Player name — the display name you choose. It is visible to other players on leaderboards, in tournaments and in multiplayer matches.
  • Game progress — levels, unlocked ships and skills, in-game currency (e.g. gold), owned cosmetic items, settings, daily missions and achievement progress, synchronized to the cloud so you can keep your progress.
  • Scores and rankings — high scores, leaderboard entries and weekly tournament results.
  • Multiplayer data — matchmaking requests, match and room identifiers, the IDs and player names of your opponent or teammate, and real-time match state (such as positions, health, score and game events) exchanged during 1v1 Cyber Duel and 2-player Co-op Operation matches.

1.2 Optional account linking

  • Google account (via Firebase Authentication): if you choose to link it, Google shares your Google account ID, name, email address and profile photo URL with Firebase Authentication so your progress can be restored on other devices.
  • Apple Game Center (iOS): if you are signed in to Game Center, we receive your Game Center player identifier and alias to link your progress, achievements and leaderboards.

1.3 Device, technical and advertising data

  • Technical data processed by our service providers when the game connects to them: IP address, device model, operating system and version, app version, language/region settings and timestamps.
  • Advertising data processed by Google AdMob when you choose to watch a rewarded ad: the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information and ad interaction data (e.g. ad shown, completed, clicked).

1.4 Purchase data

All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive or store your payment card or bank details. We receive only the information needed to verify and restore a purchase: product ID, transaction/purchase token, purchase date and status.

1.5 Information you send us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message (for example your player name, device model or screenshots).

1.6 What we do not collect

Neon Pulse does not access your precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or browsing history.

3.Services we use

3.1 Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database (Google)

Firebase provides the anonymous account ID, optional Google / Game Center account linking, cloud saves, player names, scores and leaderboard records, daily mission and achievement synchronization, and the real-time matchmaking and match state for online multiplayer. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

3.2 Google AdMob

Neon Pulse shows only user-initiated rewarded ads — for example to revive after defeat, double (×2) collected gold, refresh skill card choices, or claim a daily supply drop. An ad is loaded and shown only after you tap to watch it; there are no banner or forced interstitial ads. AdMob uses advertising identifiers and device data to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent ad fraud. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3.3 Apple Game Center, App Store and Google Play Billing

On iOS, Apple Game Center provides achievements and leaderboards, governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Cosmetic ship skins are sold as non-consumable in-app purchases through the Apple App Store and Google Play Billing; we use the transaction data they provide only to validate purchases and restore items you own.

3.4 Local notifications

Daily reward reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They do not use a push-notification server and do not send any data to us. You can turn them off at any time in your device’s notification settings or in the game’s settings.

4.Advertising choices

  • Watching ads is always optional. If you never tap a rewarded-ad button, no ad is shown.
  • iOS: the IDFA is used only if you allow it via the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (wording may vary by device).
  • Where required by law (e.g. in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent message lets you choose whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, non-personalized ads may still be shown. You can also manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

5.How information is shared

  • We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with data brokers.
  • Service providers: only with the providers listed in Section 3 (Google/Firebase, Google AdMob, Apple, Google Play), who process it to provide their services.
  • Other players: your player name, scores, rankings and in-match activity are visible to other players.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety and security of players, the game or others.

6.Data retention

  • Account, progress and leaderboard data are kept for as long as your account exists, so you don’t lose progress. Historical tournament rankings are kept until you request deletion.
  • Real-time multiplayer session data is temporary and is removed after the match ends; match results may be stored with your progress and rankings.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and then deleted.
  • After a deletion request, your data is erased from our active databases within 30 days. Residual copies in service-provider backups expire according to those providers’ retention schedules.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google in accordance with Google’s policies; purchase records are retained by Apple/Google as required by their policies and applicable law.

7.Your rights & data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, email devsupport.tc@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7.1 Reset your progress in the game

Open Settings in Neon Pulse and choose “Reset All Progress”, then confirm. This permanently erases your game progress (levels, unlocks, currency, missions and achievement progress) and cannot be undone. Cosmetic items you bought are tied to your App Store / Google Play account and can be restored with Restore Purchases.

7.2 Delete your account and all cloud data

  1. Send an email to devsupport.tc@gmail.com with the subject “Neon Pulse – Account & Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include your player name, your platform (iOS or Android) and, if available, the player ID shown in the game’s settings/profile screen, or the Google / Game Center account you linked.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the game or from the linked account to verify ownership.
  4. Within 30 days we delete your Firebase Authentication account and all associated data in Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database — progress, player name, scores, leaderboard and tournament entries, mission/achievement records — and confirm by email.

Deleting your game account does not cancel your Apple or Google account and does not automatically refund purchases (see our Terms of Use). Game Center achievements are stored by Apple and can be managed in your Apple account settings.

8.Children

Neon Pulse is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9.Security & international transfers

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS), and access to cloud data is restricted by authentication and database security rules so that players can only modify their own records. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Our service providers may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

10.Changes & contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed; material changes will be announced in the game or on this page.

Hiçbir iletim veya saklama yöntemi %100 güvenli değildir; ancak bilgilerinizi korumak için makul önlemler alırız.
